ABOUT CONCRETESTRENGTH

Good questions.
Solid foundations.

Concrete is a combination of ingredients, time, and decisions. ConcreteStrength helps you explore that relationship through machine learning, with a workspace that makes complex inputs easier to understand.

THE PURPOSE

Material data.
Made more approachable.

Built for students, researchers, and professionals exploring concrete mixtures. Bring mix inputs, predicted strength, and past results into a single organized workflow. Enter your mixture quantities and concrete age to estimate compressive strength in MPa. Revisit saved predictions to review the ingredients behind each result. Use dashboard statistics and visual analytics to explore your prediction history.

WHAT GOES INTO A PREDICTION

Eight inputs.
One useful starting point.

The dataset records each ingredient, including water and superplasticizer, in kilograms per cubic metre of mixture (kg/m³). Age is recorded in days; predicted strength is reported in MPa. The prediction form shows the supported range for each input.

01Cement (kg/m³)
02Blast furnace slag (kg/m³)
03Fly ash (kg/m³)
04Water (kg/m³)
05Superplasticizer (kg/m³)
06Coarse aggregate (kg/m³)
07Fine aggregate (kg/m³)
08Age of concrete (days)
